# Mara Oliveira

**Fictional case · 14 February 2025 · Linden Ward**

Mara, a retired seamstress who has lived above the old bakery for 38 years, received a renewal offer that would take her monthly rent from **€420 to €6,300**. That is fifteen times the previous amount. The fictional landlord declined to renew when she could not meet the new price.

Neighbors helped Mara move her plants and sewing machine to a smaller flat across town. She says the hardest part was leaving the window where she watched the street wake up each morning.

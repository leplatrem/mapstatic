# Inés and Pau

**Fictional case · 3 November 2024 · Linden Ward**

![Moving boxes in a room](images/moving-boxes.jpg)

*Illustrative CC0 photo; this is not Inés and Pau's home.*

Inés, a piano teacher, and Pau, a bicycle mechanic, shared this fictional apartment for 22 years. Their landlord declined to renew the lease after proposing a new rent roughly three times higher. The couple moved their workshops and home to a town an hour away by train.

The address shown on this map is a made-up point used only to demonstrate feature pages.
